# MoodMate --- App Logo Design

## Overview

MoodMate is a friendly mood and wellness app. Its logo uses a dreamy
pastel look to communicate calm, comfort, positivity, and emotional
support.

## Visual Concept

-   **Main symbol:** A cute cloud with closed, smiling eyes and a small
    curved mouth.
-   **Orbit ribbon:** A smooth pastel ribbon wraps around the cloud,
    suggesting connection, companionship, and changing moods.
-   **Sparkles:** Two small sparkles add a gentle, magical touch.
-   **Wordmark:** "MoodMate" appears below the symbol in a bold, rounded
    white font.

## Color Palette

The background blends soft pastel shades inspired by the supplied
reference image: - Sky blue - Mint - Cream - Blush pink - Lavender -
Lilac

Use smooth transitions between colors and avoid harsh contrast so the
overall look remains soft and welcoming.

## Shape and Layout

-   Use a rounded-square app-icon shape.
-   Keep the cloud symbol centered in the upper-middle area.
-   Place the "MoodMate" wordmark underneath, centered and clearly
    legible.
-   Leave comfortable spacing around the symbol and text.
-   Keep decorative details minimal so the logo remains recognizable at
    small sizes.

## Style Guidelines

-   Minimal, cute, soft, and modern
-   Smooth rounded edges
-   Gentle glossy highlights
-   White symbol outlines and wordmark
-   No sharp shadows, busy patterns, or extra slogans

## Brand Feel

The design should feel safe, friendly, dreamy, and supportive---like a
little companion for checking in with your emotions.

## Suggested Use

Use the full logo for splash screens, welcome pages, and promotional
graphics. For small app icons or favicons, use the cloud symbol and
pastel background, and omit the wordmark if needed for legibility.
